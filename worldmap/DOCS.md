# Worldmap

All your trips on one map. Open **Worldmap** in the sidebar.

1. **Settings › People & groups** – add single persons (with their `person.*` / `device_tracker.*` entity) and groups
   such as couple or family. Set home and work. They appear on top of every map to tap.
   **Connections** – OwnTracks (live without cloud), Google Timeline, Apple; **Notifications** – choose a phone.
2. **Import** – drop Google Timeline exports, CSV/Excel, GPX or KML files. A preview shows what was found.
3. **Questions** – answer "who was there?" for new trips.
4. **+ Trip by hand** – add old trips without any data; they appear as an estimated area on the map.

Privacy: all data stays in this add-on (`/data`, included in backups). The only downloads are OpenStreetMap
map tiles by tile number (for the rail and road network), cached afterwards.

Full documentation (English and German): https://github.com/magi1982/worldmap
